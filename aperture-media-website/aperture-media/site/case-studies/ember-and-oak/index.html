Case studies / Ember & Oak

Ember & Oak

From neighborhood secret to a full house on weeknights.

Client
Ember & Oak, wood-fired pizzeria
Services
Social media management, Video production and Reels, Photography, Influencer marketing
Platforms
Instagram, TikTok

The client

A 40-seat wood-fired pizzeria with a beautiful oven and almost no online presence.

The challenge

Weekends filled up by word of mouth, but weeknights were quiet and the account posted twice a month. Photos were taken on the fly in dim light and did not show what makes the food special.

The strategy

We built the feed around the oven: the fire, the stretch, the blister, the first slice. One content pillar per weekday gave followers something to expect and gave the kitchen a simple rhythm to film.

Content produced

  • Weekly Reels filmed at the pass and the oven
  • A rebuilt grid with a consistent look
  • Story series on the daily special
  • Full menu and dish photography
  • Creator tasting nights

Campaign execution

  1. Menu shoot

    Two half-days to photograph the full menu and capture reusable oven footage.

  2. Content calendar

    Four content pillars mapped across the week and filmed in monthly batches.

  3. Creator nights

    Local food creators hosted on quiet weeknights, briefed and tracked.

  4. Always-on management

    Posting, replies and monthly reporting handled by our team.

Results

3.4×

average Reel reach versus the quarter before

+58%

weeknight covers reported by the owner

12k

new followers in 90 days

4.1%

average engagement rate

Visuals and video

The outcome

Weeknights became a reason to visit, not a gap to fill. The restaurant now runs a monthly content rhythm the whole team understands.
