Case studies / Noodle Theory

Noodle Theory

One repeatable format that made the broth pour famous.

Client
Noodle Theory, ramen bar
Services
Video production and Reels, Video editing, Social media strategy
Platforms
TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts

The client

A ramen bar with a loyal local crowd and a kitchen that loves its craft.

The challenge

The food was compelling in person and flat online. Content was inconsistent, and nothing was built to travel beyond existing followers.

The strategy

We found one moment that sells the bowl, the broth pour, and turned it into a recognizable short-form format with a consistent hook, sound and framing.

Content produced

  • A recurring broth-pour Reel series
  • Behind-the-scenes prep clips
  • TikTok-first edits with captions and sound design
  • Stills for menu, web and delivery apps

Campaign execution

  1. Format testing

    Six hooks tested across two weeks to find what people watched to the end.

  2. Series production

    One shoot day per month delivering a full month of videos.

  3. Cross-posting

    Every video edited natively for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

  4. Refinement

    Monthly review of watch time and saves, then adjust.

Results

640k

views on the top broth-pour video

2.7×

increase in profile visits

+34%

delivery orders attributed to social

9 sec

average watch time on short-form

Visuals and video

The outcome

A simple, repeatable format gave the brand a signature look and a steady stream of new customers.
