Case studies / Common Ground Coffee

Common Ground Coffee

A brand system for five cafés that felt like one.

Client
Common Ground Coffee, coffee roaster and cafés
Services
Brand strategy and identity, Social media strategy, Content creation
Platforms
Instagram, TikTok, website

The client

A specialty coffee roaster growing from one café to five, with each location telling its own story.

The challenge

Growth had created five different voices. Menus, social posts and packaging did not look or sound like they belonged to the same brand.

The strategy

We defined the brand around a single idea, coffee as a place to meet, and wrote the guidelines for voice, visuals and content that each café could apply in its own way.

Content produced

  • Brand platform and tone of voice
  • Photo and video style guide
  • Content templates for each location
  • Launch content for two new cafés

Campaign execution

  1. Brand workshop

    Founders and café teams defined what makes the brand different.

  2. Guidelines

    A short, practical guide covering voice, visuals and content.

  3. Roll-out

    Templates and training for each location.

  4. Ongoing support

    Quarterly reviews and content for new openings.

Results

5

cafés aligned under one brand

+72%

follower growth across location accounts

3.6%

average engagement rate

2

new cafés opened with a full content launch

Visuals and video

The outcome

Five locations now look, sound and post like one brand, and new openings launch with a ready-made playbook.
