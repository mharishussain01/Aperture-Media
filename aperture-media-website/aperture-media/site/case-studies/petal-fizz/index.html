Case studies / Petal Fizz

Petal Fizz

A product launch built to be seen, saved and shared.

Client
Petal Fizz, sparkling drinks brand
Services
Photography, Video production and Reels, Social media advertising, Influencer marketing
Platforms
Instagram, TikTok, Meta Ads

The client

A new sparkling drink brand entering a crowded shelf with a fresh look and a small budget.

The challenge

A new name, no audience, and a product that needed to look as good on a phone screen as it does in the shop.

The strategy

We combined bright product photography with playful short-form video, then put paid budget behind the best-performing creative to build recognition fast.

Content produced

  • Packshot and lifestyle photography
  • Launch Reels and product teasers
  • Paid social creative in multiple cuts
  • Creator seeding kits and content

Campaign execution

  1. Look and feel

    A visual system for photography and video that matches the packaging.

  2. Launch shoot

    Studio and location shoot producing content for six weeks.

  3. Creator seeding

    Kits sent to creators with a clear, flexible brief.

  4. Paid amplification

    Testing creative variants and scaling the winners.

Results

1.9M

people reached in the launch month

2.3%

click-through rate on the best ad

14

stockists who enquired after seeing the content

8k

followers before the first restock

Visuals and video

The outcome

The brand launched with an audience, a recognizable look and content it could reuse across retail, web and social.
