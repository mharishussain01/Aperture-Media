Glaze Lab

A feed makeover that finally matched the doughnuts.

Client
Glaze Lab, doughnut shop
Services
Brand strategy and identity, Photography, Social media management
Platforms
Instagram, TikTok

The client

A doughnut shop with a devoted fanbase and a social presence that did not reflect the product.

The challenge

Photos were inconsistent, colors clashed and the feed looked different every week. The doughnuts were the star, but nothing was presented that way.

The strategy

We simplified the visual system: one background style, one lighting setup, and a content mix that alternates close-ups, process clips and behind-the-counter moments.

Content produced

  • A refreshed grid and highlight covers
  • Weekly flavor-drop photography
  • Process Reels: glazing, filling, boxing
  • A reusable set of templates

Campaign execution

  1. Feed audit

    A full review of what was working and what was not.

  2. Visual system

    A simple photography and layout style the team could keep up.

  3. Reshoot

    One day to capture the full range of current flavors.

  4. Weekly drops

    A repeatable flavor-drop schedule with coordinated content.
